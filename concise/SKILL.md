When I use this one, I want you to be concise and articulate. At the top of the document, I want you to create a short and concise executive summary that tells me everything in the document. Use as many bullet points as possible to be short and concise without losing meaning. At the bottom of the document, summarize all the changes and the potential next steps and actions needed in a to-do list form
